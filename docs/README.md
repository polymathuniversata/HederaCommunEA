# Hedera CommunEA: Gamified Community Governance

## Overview

Hedera CommunEA is an innovative project developed for the Hedera Africa Hackathon 2025, specifically targeting Sub-track 4: Immersive Experience. This initiative creates a gamified platform for community governance within the Hedera ecosystem, with a special focus on the East African community. By leveraging meme coins, reward systems, and community-owned economies, the project aims to foster active engagement and establish decentralized control mechanisms to ensure sustainable growth of the Hedera network in the region.

## Governance Model

Hedera CommunEA is structured as a **Decentralized Autonomous Cooperative (DACO)**, registered as a company limited by Guarantee in Kenya. We coin the term "DACO" to distinguish it from traditional DAOs, emphasizing cooperative principles alongside decentralization.

### Why DACO over DAO?
- **Cooperative Principles**: Combines cooperative ownership and democratic control with blockchain decentralization
- **Legal Protection**: Provides formal legal recognition and liability protection through company registration
- **African Context**: Aligns with established cooperative movements across East Africa (e.g., SACCOs in Kenya)
- **Hybrid Governance**: Enables both on-chain voting and off-chain community decision-making
- **Sustainability**: Better suited for long-term community ownership and economic viability
- **Real-World Impact**: DAO is too abstract and lacks legal standing for tangible community development

# Agile Sprint Planning

## Sprint Planning Methodology

This document outlines the agile sprint planning process used by the Hedera CommunEA development team.

## Sprint Structure

### Sprint Duration
- Standard sprint length: 2 weeks
- Sprint planning: First day of sprint
- Sprint review and retrospective: Last day of sprint

### Sprint Planning Meeting
- Duration: 2 hours maximum
- Attendees: All team members, product owner
- Activities:
  - Review sprint goal and objectives
  - Estimate user stories and tasks
  - Commit to sprint backlog
  - Identify sprint risks and dependencies

## Sprint Planning Template

### Sprint Information
- Sprint Number: [Number]
- Sprint Goal: [Brief description of sprint objective]
- Start Date: [Date]
- End Date: [Date]
- Sprint Capacity: [Total team capacity in story points/hours]

### Sprint Backlog
| User Story | Story Points | Assignee | Status |
|------------|--------------|----------|--------|
| [Story description] | [Points] | [Name] | [Status] |

### Sprint Risks & Mitigations
- Risk: [Description]
  - Mitigation: [Action plan]

### Sprint Commitments
- Team agrees to complete [X] story points
- Key deliverables: [List of must-have items]
- Stretch goals: [Nice-to-have items]

## Estimation Guidelines

### Story Point Scale
- 1: Trivial task (< 2 hours)
- 2: Small task (2-4 hours)
- 3: Medium task (4-8 hours)
- 5: Large task (1-2 days)
- 8: Complex task (2-3 days)
- 13: Very complex task (3-5 days)

### Estimation Factors
- Complexity of implementation
- Testing requirements
- Dependencies on other teams/features
- Uncertainty and risk level

## Sprint Tracking

### Daily Standups
- Time: 15 minutes, same time each day
- Format: What did you do yesterday? What will you do today? Any blockers?
- Location: Team communication channel

### Sprint Burndown
- Updated daily by scrum master
- Tracks remaining work vs. time
- Used to identify if sprint is on track

## Sprint Review & Retrospective

### Sprint Review
- Demonstrate completed work to stakeholders
- Gather feedback on product increment
- Discuss what went well and what could be improved

### Sprint Retrospective
- What went well?
- What could be improved?
- Action items for next sprint

## Sprint Cancellation

### When to Cancel
- Sprint goal becomes obsolete
- Major changes in business priorities
- Team capacity significantly reduced

### Cancellation Process
- Notify stakeholders immediately
- Move incomplete work back to product backlog
- Schedule new sprint planning meeting
